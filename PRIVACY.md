# Observer — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 31 October 2026*

This policy covers **Observer Faculty** and **Observer Student** (together, "Observer"), published by **Vidyasagar Academy**.

## Summary

Observer works only inside your own local network. It does not collect personal data, does not send anything to the internet, and has no advertising, analytics or tracking.

## What Observer handles, and where it stays

| Data | Where it goes | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Pictures of student screens | From the student PC to the teacher PC on the local network only | Not saved |
| Student names typed at sign-in | To the teacher PC on the local network | On the teacher PC |
| PC names and network-card (MAC) addresses | To the teacher PC, so it can list PCs and switch them on | On the teacher PC |
| Messages and files sent between teacher and students | Between those PCs only | In the folders the teacher or student chooses |
| Tests, answers, marks and question banks | Between those PCs only | On the teacher PC, or in a shared folder the school chooses |
| Settings and a log file for fixing problems | Nowhere | On each PC, in the app's own data folder |

Vidyasagar Academy never receives any of this data.

## Security

Every link between the PCs is encrypted (X25519 key exchange, ChaCha20-Poly1305). A student PC obeys only a teacher PC it has joined with a code or an accepted request.

## Students are told

While a teacher PC is connected, every student screen shows a strip at the top saying that the teacher can see the screen.

## Children

Observer is meant for schools and colleges. It collects no data for Vidyasagar Academy from anyone, including children. Each school decides how it uses Observer in its own labs and is responsible for informing students and parents.

## Removing data

Uninstalling Observer removes the program. Settings, marks and files saved by Observer stay in the app's data folder and the chosen folders until you delete them.
